# 0042: A women's course shows only in the women's area

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Decided by:** project owner

## Context

A rav's lesson for women stays on the general surfaces with a tag, and also appears
in the women's area. Courses needed their own answer, because the home row is short
and a course a visitor cannot join is a wasted place in it.

## Decision

A course whose audience is women appears in the women's area and nowhere on the
general surfaces, whoever teaches it. A course for men appears on the general surfaces
only. A course for both appears in both (amended by the owner on 2026-09-25).

## Consequences

- This differs from lessons on purpose. The same rav's women-only lesson and
  women-only course are found in different places.
- A women's course is never on the home row, so it depends on the women's area being
  found.
- A rav's own page shows his courses for men and for both, never his women's course. A
  rabbanit's page is women-scoped throughout, as it is for her lessons, so it shows
  hers. A place's page shows the general courses only.

## Rejected

- **The lesson rule, general surfaces with a tag.** It would spend home-row places on
  courses most visitors of that row cannot join.
