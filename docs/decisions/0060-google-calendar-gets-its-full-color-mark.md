# 0060: Google Calendar gets its full-color mark

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Decided by:** goorlavi
- **Refines:** [0027](0027-full-color-brand-marks-on-navigation-links.md)

## Context

0027 allowed full-color brand marks for Waze and Google Maps only, because a visitor
picks a navigation app by its icon. The calendar sheet
([0059](0059-the-calendar-sheet-asks-which-calendar-first.md)) now asks the same kind of
question: which calendar app do you use.

## Decision

The "יומן Google" choice carries Google Calendar's full-color mark, beside its name. It
is the third and only other brand exception; every other icon on the site stays in the
site's own palette.

## Consequences

- One more set of literal brand colors in the code, kept beside the mark as the Waze and
  Google Maps ones are.
- The device choice ("היומן בטלפון או במחשב") keeps the site's own plum calendar icon,
  since it stands for several apps, none of them one brand.

## Rejected

- **The name alone with the site's plum calendar icon.** Calmer, but the mark is how
  people recognize the app they use, which is the whole point of the choice.
