# 0040: Course gallery photos carry no restriction and no consent step

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Decided by:** project owner

## Context

A course page shows a cover and up to 8 gallery photos chosen by whoever listed the
course. Photos of a lesson in progress show people. The site has no moderation queue
and no way to know who agreed to appear.

## Decision

The site imposes nothing on what is uploaded and asks for no confirmation of consent.
What the uploader chose is on the uploader's own responsibility. This is a gap
accepted on purpose.

## Consequences

- A photo that someone objects to, on grounds of modesty, privacy, or not having
  agreed to appear, is removed by hand after the complaint, by an administrator.
- The site's standing with its audience rests on the judgment of each lister.
- **What would change this:** the first photo that draws a real complaint.

## Rejected

- **An approval queue for photos.** It would make the owner a bottleneck on every
  course, for a problem that has not happened yet.
- **A consent checkbox.** It moves no responsibility that the lister does not already
  carry, and it reads as the site distrusting the rabbi.
